## Hey hey hey! 👋

### 🔭 🌱 About me

I'm Rui, a software engineer based in Aveiro, Portugal. I hold a bachelor's and a master's degree in Computer and Telematics Engineering from the University of Aveiro, and in 2023 I completed my PhD in Information and Communication Technologies at the University of A Coruña, Spain.

I spent seven years in research, at the University of Aveiro and the University of A Coruña, working on how hospitals store and share medical images (PACS and DICOM). Then I changed course: since 2022 I've been a Senior Software Engineer at [Plex](https://www.plex.tv), building streaming apps, from social features on watch.plex.tv to community apps for Smart TVs and consoles.

I care about quality code and clean code principles. I love learning new things, helping others along the way, and taking on new challenges. On weekends I'm usually on a trail.
